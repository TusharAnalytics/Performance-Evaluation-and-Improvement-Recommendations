
Data-driven evaluation of a **hypothetical** consumer-goods distributor (all figures are illustrative).
Twelve SCOR-aligned KPIs are benchmarked, bottlenecks are diagnosed, and seven recommendations are proposed with targets, roadmap, risks and financial impact.


- `report/` - final Word report (.docx)
- `data/kpi_baseline.csv` - KPI baseline, benchmark and 12-month targets
- `images/kpi_chart.png` - KPI comparison chart
- `scripts/make_chart.py` - generates the chart (Python, matplotlib)
- `scripts/build_report.js` - builds the report (Node.js, `docx` package)

- Strength: picking accuracy (98.4%).
- Critical gaps: forecast accuracy, OTIF, supplier on-time delivery, inventory turnover, logistics cost.
- Targets: OTIF 84% to 91%, turnover 5.2x to 7.0x, logistics cost 11.5% to 9.5% of sales.

1. Statistical forecasting + S&OP
2. Supplier scorecards and dual sourcing
3. ABC-XYZ inventory segmentation
4. Transport optimisation
5. Warehouse slotting
6. Control-tower dashboard
7. Returns root-cause programme


Data and benchmarks are hypothetical and for learning purposes only.
